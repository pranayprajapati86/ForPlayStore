Privacy Policy for LevaD-DevaD: Biz, Staff, Stock

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how LevaD-DevaD: Biz, Staff, Stock (“the App”), developed by Pranay Prajapati, handles information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information About the App

App Name: LevaD-DevaD: Biz, Staff, Stock

Developer: Pranay Prajapati

Support Email: pranay.prajapati.86@gmail.com

LevaD-DevaD: Biz, Staff, Stock is a business management application that helps users manage credit/debit transactions (LevaD-DevaD), staff records, stock purchases, daily stock usage, and remaining inventory locally on their device.

2. Information Collection and Use

Core business data created or entered by you—including customer/party ledgers, credit/debit entries, staff records, item lists, stock purchases, and inventory usage—is stored locally on your device.

We do not require user registration, and we do not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal, business, or device data on external servers or with third parties.

3. Local Data Storage

The App stores user-created data locally on your device to provide core functionality, including:

Managing business transactions and credit/debit ledgers (LevaD-DevaD)

Managing staff details and records

Tracking stock purchases, daily usage, and remaining inventory

Your locally stored business data does not leave your device unless you manually export, share, or back up your data using your device’s own backup or sharing tools.

4. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The App does not display advertisements and does not integrate third-party advertising or analytics SDKs that collect or transmit user data.

5. Permissions

LevaD-DevaD: Biz, Staff, Stock only requests standard device permissions necessary for core app functionality and local storage/export.

6. Children’s Privacy

LevaD-DevaD: Biz, Staff, Stock is intended for business and general use and is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Data Deletion

Because your business, staff, and stock records are stored locally on your device:

You can delete all local app data at any time by clearing the App’s storage/data in your Android device settings or by uninstalling the App.

We do not maintain user accounts or store any user or business data on external servers.

8. Security

Because your business, staff, and inventory records are stored locally on your device, you are responsible for maintaining the security of your device and any manual backups you create.
